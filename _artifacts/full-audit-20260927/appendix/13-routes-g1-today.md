## Global shell (header / sidebar / footer) — brief

- **Sidebar nav**, grouped: 데일리 (오늘의 브리핑, 대시보드, 시장 뉴스) → 시장 분석 (시장 환경/시장 폭/투자 심리/차트·기술 분석/거시경제/환율·채권/테마·트렌드) → 내 투자·도구 (포트폴리오/기업 분석/퀀트 스크리너) → 학습 (시장 원리/대가의 포트폴리오/AI 시대 지식 지도/사용 설명서) + floating 용어사전 button. Grouping is sensible and matches the stated dual purpose (trading tools vs. learning), but **대시보드 (home)** sits oddly under "데일리" above "시장 뉴스" while "오늘의 브리핑" (also daily) sits first — the two daily-summary pages are adjacent to a third page (market-news) that duplicates much of their content (see Overlap section).
- **Header badges**: a data-coverage pill ("기준 시세 …(N개)" / "부분 LIVE … (13개)") that changed value mid-session (16→13 tickers) confirming it's live; a VIX quick value; "AI 베타" toggle; refresh button; a data-source status line (Yahoo quotes/chart, CoinGecko, RSS, FRED). This pill is the *only* place on #home that plainly shows "part of the market data isn't live," yet the big score card two inches below just says "판정 보류" without repeating why in the same words — a beginner has to connect two different vocabularies ("일부 미수신" vs "필수 입력 미수신") to understand it's the same root cause.
- **Footer**: a persistent one-line disclaimer ("투자 유의: 정보 제공용이며 최종 판단은 본인 책임입니다.") appears at the bottom of every one of these 5 routes verbatim — consistent and good practice (KEEP), through it's redundant to also show an inline "고점 대비" disclaimer-ish line at top of #home.
- **Settings drawer / API key panel / self-diagnostic checklist**: large (20-item) system self-diagnostic list, all showing "⏳" (pending/async) in the snapshot captured — could not verify it resolves; this is a power-user/operator surface, not really route content, out of scope for per-element grading but flagged as UNVERIFIED whether it ever finishes for a normal user session.

## #home — 대시보드

### Purpose
Answers "what's the market doing right now, and should I be trading?" in one glance. First screen is a decision header (환경점수 hero, blocked in observed state), then a KPI strip (S&P/NASDAQ/VIX/F&G), then a cross-asset strip (DXY/10Y/Gold/WTI/KOSPI/BTC), then "오늘의 시장" (3 headline news cards), then a collapsed "글로벌 마켓 상세" table, then an AI chat box. No inline explanation of *why* (data pipeline delay) sits next to that specific card — the reason is stated 2 sections up in a small text line and again in a collapsed `DETAILS` toggle. Audience: **both** (beginner gets a plain-language decision line; the score-component breakdown, action-item card with position-sizing math and the 결측 audit language are trader/analyst-oriented).

Cut the empty `home-operator-note` and `home-conclusion-bar` divs if truly always empty (confirm via `grep` before removing in a future change — not done here, read-only audit).

**Consolidated structure proposal**: treat these as one "market state" information architecture with distinct *jobs*, not distinct *content sets*:
1. **#home** = the single decision surface. Score hero + freshness + one link out to "why" (guide's capability manifest) + a *pointer* (not a re-render) to today's top story on #briefing.

4. **#signal** = the only place with the mode toggle, checklist, and risk monitor — genuinely distinct from home's hero, but the score component *formula legend* should be pulled from the exact same canonical source home and guide already use, eliminating the 모멘텀/심리 mismatch.

This would cut the amount of literally-duplicated news-card markup roughly in half across the three news-touching routes, and remove the redundant 3-headline block from #home entirely, shortening its already-crowded first screen.
