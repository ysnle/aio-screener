Good afternoon. The Committee decided to lower the target range for the federal funds rate by 1/4 percentage point. Inflation has eased but remains somewhat elevated, and the labor market has softened somewhat. We will make our decisions meeting by meeting, and the Committee is not on a preset course. The balance sheet runoff will continue at the current pace. Financial conditions have tightened for some businesses and households, and we will watch credit conditions closely. Thank you, and I look forward to your questions about the outlook, the labor market and the path of policy over the coming months.
